# Cynaris Data Analyst Internship — EDA on Indian Business Dataset

## Project Overview
End-to-end exploratory data analysis, BI dashboarding, statistical probability and hypothesis testing, and regression modeling on the Indian Business Dataset (`cleaned_indian_business_dataset.csv`).

### Week 4 Capstone Findings (Indian Business Dataset)
- **Descriptive & Probability Analysis:** Total tracked revenue is **₹695 Cr** across 6 companies (mean ₹115.83 Cr, median ₹110.00 Cr), with CLT and binomial simulations illustrating sampling behavior and risk modeling.
- **Regression & Correlation:** Headcount vs. revenue has a Pearson correlation of **-0.6926** (Spearman **-0.7714**); simple linear regression yields **$R^2 = 0.4798$**, **MAE = ₹37.05 Cr**, and **RMSE = ₹42.69 Cr**.
